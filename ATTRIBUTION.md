# Attribution and non-affiliation

Grainfield is an independent software instrument inspired by the Mutable Instruments Beads architecture and hardware workflow. Mutable Instruments is not affiliated with or endorsing this project. Any redistributed upstream software or artwork will retain its original author, license, and attribution requirements.

The final attribution text and links will be completed before artwork or adapted DSP is packaged. Until then, the manifest uses `pending` states rather than implying clearance.
