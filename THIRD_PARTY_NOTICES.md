Beadsy uses an authorized raster build of the Beads straight-on panel artwork
from `hardware_design/panel/beads.ai` in the local Mutable Instruments Beads
source checkout. The artwork is used by permission of the project owner for
this Beadsy build and remains attributed to Mutable Instruments. Beadsy is an
independent project and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by
Mutable Instruments.

The DSP adaptation and the Beadsy software controls are separately identified
in the source tree. The panel source is retained under
`provenance/source/beads-panel.ai`; the generated PNG is an authoring artifact
used to render the native Skia panel.
