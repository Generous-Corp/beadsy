The Meru panel is an adaptation of the Beads front panel design by
Mutable Instruments (Emilie Gillet), published in
<https://github.com/pichenettes/eurorack> under
`beads/hardware_design/panel/beads.ai` (commit
f4cfa7697095a28d292bff758beb51deb49b5a86) and licensed under the Creative
Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>).

Changes made for Meru (the license asks that they be indicated):

- the Mutable Instruments name and mark were removed and the lettering
  "MERU / MIDI GRAIN INSTRUMENT / INDEPENDENT SOFTWARE EDITION" added;
- FREEZE and SEED are drawn as large lit button faces in place of the panel
  push buttons and their LEDs;
- the quality (media) push button and its three indicator LEDs are merged
  into one colour-coded button, the colour showing the selected quality;
- the assign push button and its indicators are merged into one coloured
  control in the same way;
- the jack field at the bottom of the panel is removed, the panel is cropped
  there, and a band with the Source menu and the help button takes its place;
- the artwork was rendered to `provenance/generated/` for the native panel. The adapted artwork is distributed under the same CC BY-SA 3.0
license; it is not covered by Meru's MIT license. The original `.ai` file is
not redistributed here; it remains available upstream. Mutable Instruments is a
