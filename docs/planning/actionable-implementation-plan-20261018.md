# Beadsy actionable implementation plan

**Planning snapshot:** current Beadsy integration branch `integration/grainfield-current-integration-20261018`.
**Product scope:** independently branded, MIT-licensed Beadsy; v1 is the 48 kHz, global/mono MIDI synth path. Live input/effect, freeze-buffer serialization, polyphony, MPE, AudioSuite, and AAX remain deferred or blocked unless their own evidence closes them.

This plan is intentionally evidence-led. A receipt from an older source head is historical until regenerated at the exact integrated head. No artwork, adapted DSP, `stmlib`, or `waves.bin` is distributable until its rights and notice decision is closed.

## Current state and external dependency

- **DSP/MIDI/realtime:** 16 deterministic 48 kHz synth cases are byte-identical to the recorded source-port fixtures. The exact-head sample-accurate MIDI gate is complete: the 24-test suite passes, including note-on/note-off transitions at declared offsets 3 and 6 in an eight-sample block. The 64/256/1024 Release+Debug allocation matrix is clean, but its gate remains inconclusive because the historical 192-byte observation did not recur and has no owner backtrace. Broader source parity, overlap/retrigger/velocity/pitch-bend/pressure/sustain fixtures, effect path, source redistribution, and legal closure remain open.
- **UI/geometry:** 24 control locations are geometrically correlated between AI and DXF (mean residual 1.0113 pt; maximum 1.0765 pt). Twelve controls are source-backed and bound (five primary grain controls plus seven source-audited auxiliary pots); twelve socket regions remain provisional and non-automatable.
- **Knobs:** procedural v2 calibration is implemented (segmented black annulus, off-white cap, broad pointer), but the full-panel/photo pixel comparison remains open and rights-gated.
- **Interaction:** native editor-to-StateStore automation gesture and contextual LFO seam are proven. External DAW automation, complete LFO shape/rate/depth interaction, and host state restoration remain open.
- **Formats:** CLAP has a host probe; AU metadata/temporary registration exists but Pulp host discovery is blocked; VST3 evidence is historical/development-SDK based; LV2 host instantiation lacks `LV2_URID__map` and atom/MIDI support; standalone launch/quit passes but editor/audio/state evidence is incomplete; AAX has no SDK/validator/host evidence.
- **Importer:** PR [#10533](https://github.com/Generous-Corp/pulp/pull/10533) remains open at head `633ef485c6fa74993f9a4935fed27a3108b23f48`. Live state at the audit: `MERGEABLE` but `BEHIND`; 39 checks succeeded, 13 were skipped, and macOS Build/Test plus macOS AddressSanitizer and UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer remain in progress. Current `origin/main` is `fb53091de4dedcbae4753e2371f90263558230b3`, so the sidecar lane is not yet integrated into current main or Beadsy. The importer PR already owns the routed DesignIR/source-contract changes; no overlapping core mutation is safe while it is behind and running checks.

## Parallel ownership and integration rules

P1 asset/rights, P3 DSP, P5 routing, and P6 importer can proceed concurrently after P0. P2 depends on P1; P4 depends on P2 and P3; P4 depends on P2/P3; P6 depends on P0; P7 depends on P3/P4/P5/P6; P8 depends on all prior phases and rights closure; P9 is last. Shared Pulp importer/core edits are serial integration work and require the Vellum/source-authority route report before mutation. Every workstream claims its paths, uses a named Beadsy/Beads workspace, and reports exact worktree, commit, tests, receipts, blocker, and one next action.

## Release decision rule

Beadsy remains **blocked for release** until source/DSP, control semantics, full-panel fidelity, rights, realtime safety, claimed host formats, automation/modulation/state, and packaging receipts are current at the same exact head. A partial or historical receipt can guide work but cannot close a current gate.
